My story in short

I was a curious kid, and I still am. My parents run a family business, wholesale and manufacturing of home appliance covers, from the first floor of our house in Trichy, and I have helped with it since I was a child: sales, buying stock, and calling people to collect money. In 8th standard I ran one of our roadside Diwali shops, where we sold curtains, earned roughly ₹8,000 to ₹10,000, and bought my first phone, a Lenovo K5. That phone, and no laptop, is how I explored the world: I wanted to write lyrics for cinema, I learned to sell on Amazon, I started blogs, learned SEO and distribution, found readers on Quora, tried affiliate marketing, wrote and self-published a sci-fi book at 15, and freelanced as a social media marketer for small local brands.

In July 2021 I chose learning by doing over a traditional college and joined Zoho Schools of Learning. I became a pre-sales intern for the UK and Europe, then a partner solutions intern for the Middle East and Africa, working from Dubai. At 19 I presented at Zoho Inspire Dubai to roughly 300 to 400 partners, the youngest official presenter Zoho had had. Since July 2023 I have been a Partner Solutions Engineer. Since May 2024 I have led global partner enablement for Bigin, Zoho's CRM for small businesses, with 150+ exclusive partners, and today I also enable partners on AI. Along the way I have trained distributor and channel partners, pitched Zoho at partner meetups, BNI, GITEX, Coffee with Xscale and Advantage Vidarbha, and carried partner feedback to 55+ Zoho product teams.

Outside work I build. I made two open-source MCP servers that connect Claude to Indian stocks and mutual funds, and MCP servers for Zoho products. I record a podcast, Lokey thoughts. I volunteer at independent Tamil music concerts. I travel, and I plan trips that friends still ask me about. Every place on the globe is a version of me.

Yercaud on foot: nearly 30,000 steps

Salem

After the event, I had one day left before heading back to Chennai. Instead of spending it resting, I decided to visit Yercaud.

Nearly 30,000 steps later, I had experienced Yercaud in a way I probably wouldn't have if I had travelled by car.

I walked through its roads, took in the mountain views, observed people going about their lives and stopped to have conversations. I also got an opportunity to interact with local businesses and understand a little more about the people and business culture of Salem through my BNI connections.

A free day, a fort, and a ride home with strangers

Mumbai

I had a free day before my meeting, so I decided to explore.

It was a weekend, and I took a train to Nashik. From there, I made my way to a fort for a trek. My original plan was simple: finish the trek and return by train or local transport.

While trekking, I struck up conversations with a few strangers. By the time we reached the end, I somehow found myself hitchhiking back with them instead of following my original plan.

It was spontaneous, a little thrilling and genuinely fun.

Sometimes, the best part of exploring isn't the destination or the plan. It's the people you meet and the unexpected turns along the way.

Advantage Vidarbha 2025: a few facts

Nagpur

Advantage Vidarbha 2025 was an industrial expo held in Nagpur from 7 to 9 February 2025. Here is what was reported about it.

  • Organiser: the Association for Industrial Development (AID).
  • Venue: the campus ground of Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj University, Amravati Road, Nagpur.
  • Size: more than 300 stalls, of which 100 were reserved for micro and small enterprises.
  • Who took part: chief guests included Union Minister Nitin Gadkari and Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis, and exhibitors included government bodies such as SEBI, the GST Department, the Department of Posts and the Government e-Marketplace (GeM).
  • Afterwards: exhibitors reported a surge in visitor turnout, and several start-ups seeking investment reported meetings with potential investors.

For a small-business CRM, a room built for micro and small enterprises is the right place to be.
